# Code of conduct

PixelHelm contributors must keep collaboration respectful, specific, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Critique work and evidence, not people.
- Respect privacy, attribution, accessibility, and licensing boundaries.
- Disclose uncertainty and conflicts of interest.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, and publication of
  another person's private information.
- Follow the repository's approval gates; urgency does not authorize an outward or
  destructive action.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and may restrict participation
for behavior that violates these expectations. Report conduct concerns through an
established private owner channel. Do not place sensitive conduct reports in a public
issue. Before public participation opens, the owner must confirm a durable private
enforcement contact path.
