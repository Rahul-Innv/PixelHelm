How we grow it

Soak seeds overnight, then sow on edge in deep pots. It is slow to start and then rampant. Give it wire or trellis and it will do the rest.

Violet bell flowers to 6m by late summer, and it needs a warm start indoors, which is why the packet lives on the cold bench of the glass house. Do not be discouraged by the quiet first month.

Grows well alongside
