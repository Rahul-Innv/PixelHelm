How we grow it

Soak seeds overnight; sow on edge in deep pots. Slow to start, then rampant. Give it wire or trellis and it will do the rest.

Violet bell flowers to 6m by late summer; it needs a warm start indoors, which is why the packet sits in the winter drawer of the bench. Do not be discouraged by the quiet first month.

Grows well alongside

    Pale cream at the vine's feet; edible leaves and flowers.

    Blazing orange against the violet bells; butterflies take both.
