# E4-R2: permanently deferred (owner decision, 2026-07-27)

The judging-seat re-validation will not be attempted again. This closes the
question in writing rather than leaving it open indefinitely.

## The decision

**Owner (Rahul), 2026-07-27:** "considering we don't have anything to test it
against, we can say it's permanently deferred… the number one thing is the
person or the individual states itself, because for me it's different, for
somebody else also it's different."

**Standing consequence, now permanent unless real-audience evidence arrives:**
the model judging seat is ADVISORY-ONLY. No quality claim in this repository
may rest on panel scores alone.

## Why this is the honest end state, not an unfinished item

1. **There is no ground truth to validate against.** E4 measured agreement
   between two model panels sharing one constructed standard. That measures
   inter-context consistency, never validity. Passing it would not have shown
   the seat tracks real quality, and the sealed sheet always said so.
2. **The remaining fix could manufacture a pass.** E4-R failed with ZERO
   discordant pairs: the panels never disagreed, they simply could not
   discriminate (nearly every candidate scored 9.0, tying 8 of 12 pairs).
   Forcing no-tie rankings would compel the panel to break ties it cannot
   perceive, so the statistic could clear its threshold on noise. Lifting a
   demotion on a statistical artifact is precisely the failure mode this
   program exists to prevent.
3. **The standard is owner-relative.** This is the owner's own point and it is
   the deepest one: taste is not a universal quantity a panel can be validated
   against, because the referent differs per person. A seat calibrated to one
   owner's judgment is not thereby correct for another's. Chasing a universal
   judging score is chasing something that does not exist.

## What replaces it

**Per-owner calibration, accumulated from real work.** The capability ledger
(`.pixelhelm/capability/`) records the owner-versus-panel delta on every run
where both sides are measured. That is live calibration evidence from actual
projects rather than synthetic re-judging, and it accrues automatically. The
current record, from the runs to date: -1, -2, -3, -3 against panel winner
medians of 9.0, with two of the owner's bands stated as upper bounds, so the
true gap is at least that large and is not fully known.

The remaining path to genuine validity is unchanged and unrelated to panels:
**E5** (a small human probe with recruited participants) or real deployment
with captured behaviour. Both are owner-gated and neither is scheduled.

## Scope of this deferral

Deferred: E4-R2 and any further panel-versus-panel calibration experiment.
NOT deferred and still in force: the repair itself (R1 floor-clean precondition,
R2 panels receive the floor outputs), which shipped and is evidenced as working
— 4 of 4 fresh plants eliminated before any esteem score existed, and 20 of 20
jurors marked the floor-failing candidate UNSCORED and named the correct gate.
