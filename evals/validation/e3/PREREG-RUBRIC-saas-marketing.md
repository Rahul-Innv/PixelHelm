# E3 pre-registered rubric — SaaS/product-marketing archetype (Ledgerline)

**Status: DRAFT — PENDING OWNER APPROVAL. Sealed by commit before any E3
generation.** Date drafted: 2026-07-26. Archetype-specific per RF-8.
**Evidence label (RF-1/RF-4):** all scores are *system-esteem* — never
user-outcome evidence, never ground truth.

## What gets scored

All three arms' final candidates; the PASS bar applies to the winner.
Precondition: every shipped HARD Layer-1 gate green + 4-mutant ritual recorded.

## Criteria (each 0–10, integers)

Section A — fundamentals:

1. **Ten-second comprehension.** The first screenful answers what it is and who
   it is for, in the audience's language; value is communicated before any
   scroll-dependent device.
2. **Credibility engineering.** Every on-page claim traces to the sealed data
   WITH its basis where the data requires one (the pilot-estimate label); zero
   marketese; restraint reads as confidence.
3. **Conversion-path respect.** The trial CTA is unmistakable, honest ("no card
   required" only because the data says so), and never nagging; pricing is
   transparent with the VAT note wherever a price appears.
4. **Audience fit of IA and language.** Structured for a subscription-skeptic
   sole trader on a phone; plain trade language, no startup idiom.
5. **Craft discipline.** Spacing rhythm, alignment, type hierarchy (~3 sizes),
   limited palette; nothing arbitrary.
6. **Data honesty surface.** The beta integration is labeled beta; the
   unverified testimonial is omitted or explicitly labeled; nothing rounds an
   estimate into a fact.

Section B — archetype-true distinction:

7. **Signature idea.** One sentence that content, visuals, and interaction all
   reinforce, true of THIS product (ideal form: the page *demonstrates* the
   product's promise rather than asserting it).
8. **Novelty budget.** Mechanics conventional; at most 1–2 deliberate breaks,
   brand layer only, matching the arm's A1 declared-breaks block.
9. **Voice.** Copy a competitor could not publish unchanged — tradesperson-
   respecting, specific, unhyped.
10. **The page keeps the product's promise.** The product sells "your time is
    valuable"; the page itself must be fast to scan, fast to decide, nothing
    performative — form enacting the pitch.

Anchors: 0–2 broken · 3–4 below professional · 5–6 professional baseline ·
7–8 strong professional · 9–10 distinctive-excellence signal (system-esteem).

## Evaluator rule

Identical to E1's sealed rule: 5 model jurors, fresh contexts, blind, arms
shuffled under neutral labels (seeds recorded); inputs = this sheet, the sealed
brief + data, the full render matrix, Layer-1 gate outputs — nothing else. One
JSON per juror per arm via the judge-record machinery. Per-criterion median;
overall = median of the 10 criterion medians.

## PASS bar (proposed to match the owner-set E1 bar; owner may adjust before seal)

1. precondition above; 2. **no criterion median below 6**; 3. **overall median
≥ 8**. No verdicts, scores, or anticipated results exist anywhere before the
panel runs.
